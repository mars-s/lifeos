1. Preservation of compatible edits and sensible explicit conflict resolution.
2. Causal correctness: stale bases, ABA, retries, crashes, immutable receipts, no resurrection.
3. Fit to actual Things APIs and current LifeOS boundaries, honest guarantee limits.
4. Simplicity, migration safety and lightweight event-driven runtime.
5. Clear predictable user experience and concrete adversarial verification.
